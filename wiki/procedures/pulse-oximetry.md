## When to use

Estimates oxygen saturation in any patient with a breathing complaint. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Steps

Place the probe on a warm finger, wait for a steady waveform, and read the value.

## Common errors

Cold fingers, nail polish, and carbon monoxide can give false readings.

## Documentation

Record the reading along with whether the patient was on oxygen.
