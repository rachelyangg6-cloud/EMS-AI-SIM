## When to use

Any patient with an altered mental status or a diabetic history. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Steps

Clean the fingertip, lance it, and apply blood to the test strip.

## Common errors

Using the first drop contaminated with alcohol can throw off the reading.

## Documentation

Record the value and the time.
