## Dispatch
Fifty-eight-year-old male, acting confused, at an office building.

## Presentation
A man sits at his desk, pale and sweating, answering questions slowly. A coworker says he is diabetic, skipped lunch, and has been "not himself" for twenty minutes. He can hold his head up and swallow when asked.

## Vitals
RR 18, pulse 104, SpO2 97%, BP 138/86.

## Correct actions
1. Ensure the scene is safe and take Standard Precautions.
2. Assess his mental status with AVPU and confirm he has an open airway.
3. Check his blood glucose.
4. Confirm he can swallow, then administer oral glucose, one tube between the cheek and gum.
5. Obtain a SAMPLE history, including his insulin dose and last meal.
6. Reassess mental status and recheck the blood glucose.
7. Transport and give a report to the receiving hospital.

## Rationale
Confusion in a known diabetic who missed a meal is low blood sugar until proven otherwise. Oral glucose is safe only while the patient can swallow — if he stops following commands, nothing goes by mouth and ALS is needed.

## Red flags
- Cool, pale, sweaty skin
- Declining level of consciousness
- Unable to swallow
