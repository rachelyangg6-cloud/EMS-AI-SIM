## Indications

Low oxygen saturation, difficulty breathing, or signs of shock. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Adult dose

Nasal cannula at 2–6 L/min, or a nonrebreather mask at 12–15 L/min.

## Pediatric dose

Same devices, sized for the child.

## Route

Inhaled.

## Contraindications

None in an emergency.

## Scope boundary

EMT-B.
