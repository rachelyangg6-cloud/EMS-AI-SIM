## Indications

Suspected low blood sugar in a patient who can swallow. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Adult dose

One tube.

## Pediatric dose

One tube, per protocol.

## Route

By mouth, between the cheek and gum.

## Contraindications

Unable to swallow or protect the airway.

## Scope boundary

EMT-B.
