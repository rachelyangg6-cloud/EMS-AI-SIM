## Indications

Wheezing from narrowed airways in a patient with a prescribed inhaler. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Adult dose

Per the prescription and medical direction.

## Pediatric dose

Per the prescription and medical direction.

## Route

Inhaled.

## Contraindications

Not the patient's own prescription.

## Scope boundary

EMT-B may assist with the patient's own inhaler.
