## Recognition

Narrowed, inflamed lower airways cause wheezing, a prolonged exhale, and increased work of breathing. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Differentials

COPD, anaphylaxis, and fluid in the lungs can also cause wheezing or difficulty breathing.

## Assessment

Watch the work of breathing, count words per breath, listen to both sides of the chest, and check [[pulse-oximetry]].

## Field management

Sit the patient upright, give [[oxygen]], and assist with the patient's prescribed [[albuterol]] per medical direction.

## When to escalate (ALS)

A quiet chest despite hard work, a falling level of consciousness, or exhaustion means breathing is failing.
