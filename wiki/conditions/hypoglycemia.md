## Recognition

Low blood sugar, often in a diabetic patient who took medication and missed a meal. Look for confusion, pale sweaty skin, and a rapid pulse. Demo content for the open-source release. Not clinical guidance; follow your local protocols.

## Differentials

Stroke, head injury, intoxication, and seizure can look similar.

## Assessment

Check mental status, then perform a [[blood-glucose-check]].

## Field management

If the patient can swallow and protect their airway, give [[oral-glucose]]. Recheck glucose and mental status.

## When to escalate (ALS)

Any patient who cannot swallow safely, or who does not improve, needs ALS.
